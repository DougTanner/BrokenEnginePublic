# /Projects/BrokenEngineSandbox/Source/Frame/Collections/Missiles/

Guided homing missiles with area-of-effect damage. Each live missile owns a client-only area light, smoke trail, and sound.

## Game-Specific Behavior

- **Two-phase homing**: An initial boost ramps rotation strength from zero to full over a short delay; active tracking then orients toward the target with exponential smoothing. Targetless missiles orient toward the stored direction and re-attempt acquisition every Tick via `Frame::GetMissileTarget` (same path used at spawn); homing on a freshly-acquired target engages the next Tick to match the read-previous / write-current pattern. The boost-ramp delay is preserved across re-acquisition (no spawn-style soft launch on re-target).
- **Lifetime and falling**: At 10 seconds a live missile releases its target, removes its smoke trail, exhaust light, and looping sound, then preserves XY velocity while accelerating downward at 9.8 units/s². Falling disables propulsion, homing, rotation, jitter/exhaust RNG, and target acquisition. The accumulated lifetime and XYZ velocity cross cell boundaries; arrival derives falling state from the lifetime and does not hydrate client effects.
- **Area damage model**: Missiles deal zero direct collision damage — all damage flows through the single AoE explosion registered in Explode, using the constant radius/damage from `HealthDamage.h`. Collision always registers per-element damage 0; exploding entries use `kAlreadyCollided` (stay in the layer without hitting anything during the death animation), others `kDestroyOnCollide`.
- **Earliest impact resolution**: A non-exploding missile resolves one earliest event across swept entity collision, point-swept terrain impact, and frame-boundary transfer. Entity and terrain impacts move the missile to the resolved position before explosion and trail sync; terrain and boundary events win exact-time ties through the collision layer's exclusive cutoff. Falling entity and above-sea-level terrain impacts explode normally; a falling terrain hit at or below sea level silently removes the missile in the same tick without effects or area damage.
- **Target subscription lifetime**: Each Update tick validates target liveness against the current paired Targets collections while sampling the prior-frame position for homing. Validation failure, falling, explosion, transfer, or destroy releases the subscription and clears the missile id; target removal is idempotent if the row is already absent.
- **Explode is idempotent**: Guarded by a `kExploding` early-out. Releases the target immediately, plays a client one-shot explosion sound, spawns three simultaneous explosions (full/half/quarter scale, position jitter on the smaller two), registers AoE via `engine::AreaDamage::Add`, and removes area light and looping sound immediately — an ordinary live-missile smoke trail persists through the destroy animation. Directional (terrain) hits sample the cell's terrain normal for hemispherical trail/particle spread.
- **AreaDamage() phase is intentionally empty**: AoE registration happens inside Explode at hit time (PostCollision). The phase exists only to satisfy the collection interface.
- **Destroyed-time sentinel**: `-1` = alive, `> 0` = death-animation countdown (decayed in Interpolate Update, scales render size), `0` = finished — render skips the entry and Destroy removes it.
- **Copied state** (the unconditional-store exception noted at the [Collections hub](../AGENTS.md)): State forwarded by `AllocateAndCopy` is not re-stored by Update. Mutate it only at explicit lifecycle transitions (fall, explosion, transfer, or silent despawn) or when releasing client-owned effects.
- **Client-only consumers, shared RNG**: Live-missile pitch (spawn-time) and exhaust length (re-randomized every PostRender tick) feed only client audio/visuals but live in `SharedMembers()` (CRC'd) and draw from the shared frame random engine — both builds must draw identically, so their tuning constants stay `constexpr`, never runtime-tweakable. Falling missiles make no such draws.
- **Smoke-trail reuse across cell transfer**: A live missile's smoke-trail id is carried in `TransferData` so the destination cell re-binds the existing visual trail via `ClientInit`. Area lights and sounds are re-created fresh on live arrival; falling arrivals create none.
- **Engine sound + muzzle one-shot**: Each missile carries one looping engine voice on the Sounds collection (synced to the missile each frame via `Sync`). The launch cue is a fire-and-forget `gpAudioManager->PlayOneShot3d` fired once at the muzzle in `PlayersPostRender::SpawnMissiles` (Players/PlayersCombat.cpp) — not tracked in the Sounds collection, so cross-cell `TransferRequest` re-spawns do not retrigger it.
