Replace CMFT with cmgen (Filament)
===================================

Context
-------
CMFT (BSD-2) is used in the DataPacker for offline IBL cubemap processing -- generating irradiance and pre-filtered environment maps. CMFT hasn't been actively developed since ~2016. cmgen (Apache-2), part of Google's Filament project, is actively maintained and produces high-quality IBL cubemaps.

Why
---
- CMFT is effectively unmaintained (last activity ~2016)
- cmgen is actively maintained by Google as part of Filament
- Produces high-quality IBL with modern techniques
- Apache-2 license permits commercial use

Changes
-------

1. ThirdParty/
   - Add cmgen (either as submodule of Filament or extract the tool)
   - Remove cmft submodule (after migration complete)

2. DataPacker vcxproj
   - Update include paths and library references

3. DataPacker/Source/ (GenerateIrradianceCubemaps, GeneratePreFilteredCubemaps)
   - Replace CMFT API calls with cmgen equivalents
   - cmgen may be used as an external tool (CLI) or as a library
   - Map cubemap format and mip chain output to match current .pack expectations

Notes
-----
- cmgen's output format may differ from CMFT -- verify IBL quality in-engine
- CMFT bundles bx as a dependency; removing it cleans up ThirdParty
- Lower priority than tinyexr/fastgltf since CMFT is working
