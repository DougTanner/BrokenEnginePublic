# Water/ - Ocean Water Shaders

Vertex-animated ocean surface with Gerstner wave simulation, power-lobe skybox specular, and depth-based coloring.

## Overview

Tessellated quad covering the visible area. A compute pre-pass bakes Gerstner displacement and the full Jacobian wave normal into two RGBA16F textures, one texel per vertex of the active LOD grid; the vertex shader reads them via `texelFetch`. Fragment shader composites depth-LUT color, skybox reflection, directional lighting, shadows, specular, and additive smoke. Normal and noise UVs use camera-relative positions combined with CPU-reduced double-precision origins to survive far-from-origin floating-point precision.

## Architecture Notes

- **Shore-fade asymmetry**: amplitude fade from terrain elevation is applied only to low-frequency waves; medium waves run at full amplitude everywhere.
- **Zoom-out amplitude fade**: CPU populates Gerstner geometric amplitudes (low- and medium-frequency) scaled by a camera-eye-height factor that fades to zero across per-band start/end tweak sliders. Applied after steepness clamps so the steepness invariant is preserved. Eliminates aliasing of submillimeter wave geometry at far zoom; shader is unaware. At the fade end (scale clamps to 0), CPU also writes 0 into the matching `iWater*Count` so the compute shader's Gerstner loop short-circuits — no per-wave array entries are populated for that band.
- **Z flattening near shore / over land**: in the shore band (`-fWaterTerrainHeight ≤ terrain < 0`) world-Z is rescaled by `-terrain / fWaterTerrainHeight` so waves taper against the beach. Over land (`terrain ≥ 0`) the vertex shader skips the displacement fetch and emits a flat vertex with up-normal — the Z scale would collapse it anyway (the compute pre-bake now skips waves for those texels — `WaterDisplacement.comp` early-returns for `fTerrainElevation >= 0.0f`, storing zero displacement and an up-normal). No vertex cull; the fragment shader `discard`s where terrain elevation exceeds a small early-out tweak.
- **Varying conventions**: camera-relative initial position drives normal/noise UV math; fully-displaced world position drives lighting/shadow/smoke world-space lookups; a visible-area texcoord varying drives object-shadow sampling (elevation and shadow lookups recompute the texcoord from the displaced position).
- **Precision-safe sampling pact** (normals and color noise): `textureGrad` with derivatives of the camera-relative position and `fract()`-wrapped UVs — derivatives are taken before per-octave/per-sample scaling so mip selection stays stable across summed terms. Relies on the CPU-side reduced-origin modulus being chosen so per-sample size multipliers stay integer after wrap (see [Render/CLAUDE.md](../../../Source/Graphics/Render/CLAUDE.md)). Continuous tweak sliders that feed these multipliers are constructed with a snap step so user adjustments cannot break the integer-product invariant. Normal source is BC5; Z reconstructed in-shader as `sqrt(saturate(1 - x*x - y*y))` with intentionally sign-inverted XY (matches the per-island flip convention upstream).
- **Per-sample normal rotation**: each normal sample has its own rotation angle — a UV rotation applied to the camera-relative position and its derivatives, and the inverse rotation un-rotating the sampled normal XY back into world frame. The reduced origin is already rotated on the CPU (same angle, before fmod) and must not be rotated again in-shader: that would double-rotate and make the wrap shift non-integer, breaking the `fract()` precision pact. The reduced-time scroll delta is likewise pre-rotated CPU-side (by its own per-sample Speed Direction angle γ = φ − θ, not the origin's θ — see the Render hub) and must not be rotated in-shader either.
- **Multi-sample normal composition**: Several independently-parameterized samples (each a runtime-selected atlas slot, own size / reduced origin / reduced time / octave stack) are weighted-summed then normalized — `normalize()` is scale-invariant so absolute weights only matter as ratios, and a zero-vector epsilon guards NaN when all weights resolve to zero. Per-sample weights are resolved CPU-side to a single float each — `LightingUniforms.cpp` lerps between the min/max tweak endpoints by camera eye height (`engine::LerpAtHeight`, hard-coded fade band) and uploads `fWaterNormalWeight{One,Two,Three}`; the shader reads them directly (no in-shader lerp, per the Render hub's Camera-Height-Conditional Uniforms rule), letting close vs. far zoom favor different scales without a separate pipeline. Each sample group is wrapped in `if (fWeight* > 0.0f)` — the weight is a uniform, so the branch is warp-coherent and a fully-faded band skips its 3 `textureGrad` fetches.
- **Gerstner normal — full Jacobian, not simplified**: the compute pre-bake builds tangent `T = ∂P/∂u` and bitangent `B = ∂P/∂v` accumulators and emits `normalize(cross(T, B))`. The GPU Gems Eq. 12 simplified form `N.z = 1 - Σ Q·ω·A·sin` is a first-order Taylor truncation that flips negative once the Finch invariant Σ Q·ω·A → 1, so `normalize()` produces downward normals at high steepness. The full Jacobian keeps the Q² cross-term `b²` and only fails at genuine surface-cusp formation — the physically correct boundary. Matches Catlike Coding / LYGIA.
- **Unit-preserving `reflect()` (skip the redundant `normalize()`)**: the skybox-reflection direction `reflect(f3ToEyeNormal, f3SkyboxWaveNormal)` and the nested specular reflect `reflect(f3BiasedSunNormal, reflect(...))` feed the cubemap fetch / `dot()` directly without re-normalizing — both inputs are unit (`f3ToEyeNormal` and `f3SkyboxWaveNormal` are normalized where composed), so the `reflect` result stays unit. This is Water's instance of the hub's unit-preserving-identities rule (see [../CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)).
- **Source-side wave-normal dampener**: a single global blend slider mixes the Gerstner-derived normal toward flat `(0,0,1)` before it feeds any downstream consumer. Acts at the source so lighting, reflection, EWNS, and specular all see the dampened normal consistently.
- **Displacement compute pre-bake**: a compute shader (`WaterDisplacement.comp`) runs once per frame before the swapchain render pass and writes Gerstner displacement and the full Jacobian normal into two RGBA16F textures, one texel per vertex of the active LOD grid. Grid dimensions arrive via `iWaterActiveQuadX/Y` so the texture is in 1:1 alignment with the vertex grid; the vertex shader reads with `texelFetch(..., gl_VertexIndex-derived i2Grid, 0)` — no filtering, no UV math. Per-frame layout cycles `kShaderReadOnly → kComputeReadWrite → kShaderReadOnly`. The compute shader early-returns for over-land texels (`fTerrainElevation >= 0.0f`), storing zero displacement and an up-normal (the vertex shader ignores both anyway). For in-water texels it sums both low and medium bands and short-circuits per-band when the respective amplitude is zero; both bands also run inside the shore Z-taper region. The water-mesh-recreate path (`DestroyFlags::kWaterMesh`, triggered by `gWaterShapeDetail`) recreates both the mesh and these textures together so dimensions stay paired.
- **Decoupled sampled-normal blends**: separately, the downstream wave-vs-sampled-normal blends for lighting and for skybox reflection sampling are independent, letting the two use different smoothness.
- **Specular antialiasing (analytic, no sample shading)**: all three skybox-specular power lobes are evaluated inline in `Water.frag`; the sub-pixel-narrow high-power One lobe (power ~200) is de-flickered by an analytic filter selected at compile time via `WATER_SPEC_AA_MODE` at the top of the file (edit + repack, like `DT_LIGHTING_ONLY`): 0 = off/pointwise baseline, 1 = closed-form box filter of `s^p` over the `fwidth` footprint, 2 = NDF variance widening from normal screen-space derivatives (Kaplanyan/Tokuyoshi via the Vlachos production form), 3 = octave-agreement Toksvig using the pre-normalize weighted-sum length (the shipping default — best look with slightly better performance than 2; measures inter-wave disagreement, not footprint mip variance — BC5 Z-reconstruction discards the latter), 4 = 2x2 analytic supersample at derivative-extrapolated normals (quality reference, ~4x lobe ALU, zero extra fetches). Runtime sliders: `fWaterSpecAAVariance` tunes modes 1-3, `fWaterSpecAAThreshold` modes 2-3; modes 2/3 share an energy-conserving power remap `p' = 2/(2/(p+2) + kernel) − 2` with amplitude x `(1+p')/(1+p)`. Chosen over a 4x-MSAA + full-sample-shading pre-pass, which would duplicate the 9-fetch normal composition per sample and cost more than it saves.
- **Specular zoom stability (mip-variance handoff)**: screen-space kernels are blind to variance the mip chain already averaged away (BC5 + Z-reconstruction re-unitize every fetch), which flickers under camera zoom. `WATER_SPEC_AA_MIP_HANDOFF` (default 1, modes 2/3) restores it: DataPacker bakes per-mip Toksvig slope variance `mean((1−|avgN|)/|avgN|)` into `TextureHeader::pfMipVariance` for regular-path BC5 textures; `TextureManager` copies the water-normal tables at startup and `LightingUniforms` uploads the three selected octave groups' slices; the shader recomputes each octave's fetch LOD analytically from the `SAMPLE_NORMAL_PRECISE` gradients, lerps the table, weights groups by squared normalized weights, and adds `2×variance` into the mode 2/3 kernel (Bruneton-style geometry→BRDF transition, ALU only). `WATER_SPEC_AA_FADE_HANDOFF` (default 0) additionally hands over the variance the camera-height weight fade removes, referenced to near-camera full weights (`fWaterNormalWeightFull*`) — far water stays rough instead of flattening to gloss. Runtime sliders: `fWaterSpecAAMipScale` scales the term; `fWaterNormalMipBias` biases both the analytic LOD and the dedicated water-normal sampler (`kSamplerMirroredRepeatWater` — its own bias instead of the global `-gMipLodBias` sharpen; sampler recreate on change).
- Fresnel uses a `(1 - cosθ)^4` falloff (not Schlick's canonical `^5`) — intentional visual tuning.
- **Wave-trough height darken**: crest-Z is mapped to a darkening multiplier (full brightness at/above a top Z, max darken at/below a bottom Z toward a target floor). Two independent source-vs-lighting weights mix that multiplier toward 1.0 per path, so the base color and the lighting path (skybox specular + EWNS lighting deposit) can each opt in or out of the effect.
- Base-height projection projects the undisplaced world position down to the lighting plane for both EWNS lighting sampling and the smoke texcoord, keeping lighting stable under wave animation. EWNS sampling (3-sample directional, used by `WaterLighting`) optionally blends in a reflected projection — the eye→water ray reflected about the water normal, projected to the same base-height plane — to suggest sky/sun light bouncing off the surface. The reflected projection clamps and power-compresses the XY offset beyond a falloff-start radius (so heavily-bent normals don't sample hundreds of world units away) and blends in a Fresnel weight. A separate additive ambient term samples the precomputed `mAmbientCombineTexture` once at the unreflected projection (replacing three EWNS samples) to mirror the terrain shader's ambient path; the smoke blend reuses the same single ambient fetch via `BlendSmokePrecomputed` (multiplied by 4 to recover the un-averaged sum), so smoke is decoupled from the wave normal entirely — matching `Terrain.frag`.
- Hue-preserving pow on base-height lighting samples blends luminance-based and average-based exponents via a mode uniform, applied before lighting.
- Time-of-day multiplier applies linearly after lighting's internal pow, scaling output without re-exponentiation.
- **Per-target sun/moon intensity scaling**: `f4SunColor` and `f4MoonColor` arrive at the shader unscaled by any global intensity. Each shader applies its own per-target sun and moon scalars at the read site, so terrain, water, objects, and smoke can be tuned independently without re-uploading colors. The water shader scales its sky-disk sun/moon contributions by the water-target scalars; the EWNS-deposited paths (`WaterLighting()` and `AmbientLightingPrecomputed()`) intentionally bypass these scalars. The moon floor color uploads as white with a blue-tint slider boosting the blue channel; brightness control stays with the per-target moon scalars.
- **Sky-disk combine**: sun and moon contributions are combined channel-wise as `max(f4SunColor.xyz, f4MoonColor.xyz)` (variable `f3SunOrMoon`) rather than summed. The sun and moon share `f4SunMoonNormal` — they're the same celestial body alternating profiles — so summing during transitional overlap (which the moonrise/moonset window can be tuned wider than sunset/sunrise) produces unphysical doubling. The same `max` idiom is applied wherever sun/moon contributions combine in `Model.frag` (BRDF + IBL specular), `ShaderFunctions.h::SunLighting`, and the smoke ambient floors.
- **Sun/moon split shadow**: `f4SunColor` and `f4MoonColor` are uploaded as separate uniforms; the moon component bypasses the terrain ray-march shadow (object shadows + smoke volumetric attenuation still apply to both). The shadow is applied as a **scalar luminance-weighted blend** `(fShadowSun*W_sun + fShadowMoon*W_moon) / max(epsilon, W_sun+W_moon)` where the weights are Rec.601 luma over each color. A per-channel formulation would zero entire channels when `Sun.c + Moon.c` collapses to ~0 — notably morning sun has B=0 by design, and the skybox-cubemap mix in `f3LightingColor` breaks linearity in (Sun + Moon), so a per-channel divide cannot be undone by the colour multiplication. Scalar weighting is approximate but channel-safe.
- **Per-component sun/ambient shadow split (Affect Ambient slider)**: the per-target sunlight scalar carries `fSunScalar = mean(f3SunOrMoon)` and `fAmbientScalar = mean(f4AmbientColor)` separately rather than as a single summed scalar. At the shadow-apply site the sun-driven energy and the skybox specular keep the full `fEffectiveShadow`; the sky-ambient half is multiplied by `mix(1.0, fEffectiveShadow, fShadowAffectAmbient)` — same `(1-AA)+AA*fAmbientShadow` rule as `SunLighting()` in `ShaderFunctions.h`, reusing `fEffectiveShadow` directly because water's existing formula is byte-identical to `SunLighting()`'s `fAmbientShadow`. At slider=1 ambient is gated by the same shadow as the sun term, so AA=1 + occluded shadow drives the surface to black (modulo the additive EWNS+ambient deposit added after the shadow apply, which is by design unshadowed because the deposit textures hold only emitter light).
- Output alpha encodes water transparency from terrain elevation, clamped to a minimum to keep deep water opaque.
